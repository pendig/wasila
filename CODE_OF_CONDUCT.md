# Code of Conduct

Wasila follows a simple contributor standard: be respectful, constructive, and focused on making the project useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Avoid sharing private customer data, secrets, or sensitive business information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publicly posting private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical or product discussions.
- Using the project to collect, expose, or misuse customer data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate issues, discussions, pull requests, or comments that violate this code of conduct.

For now, report concerns privately to the maintainers once a public maintainer contact is available.
